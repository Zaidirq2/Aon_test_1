Alignment Lab

Seven interactive modules on sequence alignment for fourth-year biotechnology students: from why we align sequences to BLAST and multiple alignment. Each module explains, lets you experiment, sets exercises, and ends with a short quiz.

1 Why align?Why position-by-position comparison fails, gaps, mutations in HBB, dot plots. 2 Scoring basicsMatch, mismatch and gap scores; linear versus affine gaps; global, semi-global and local alignment. 3 Needleman-WunschGlobal alignment by dynamic programming, cell by cell, with traceback and co-optimal paths. 4 Smith-WatermanLocal alignment: the zero floor, traceback from the maximum, global versus local on one pair. 5 Scoring matricesBLOSUM62 built from log-odds, the full matrix explored, PAM versus BLOSUM, protein scoring. 6 BLASTWords, seeds, extension, HSPs, neighbourhood words and E-values in a step-by-step simulator. 7 Multiple alignmentReading a real globin MSA, progressive alignment with a guide tree, sum-of-pairs scoring.

Work through the modules in order; each one builds on the previous. Everything runs in the browser, on a phone or a computer, with no installation.
